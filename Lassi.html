Defination: Punjabi Lassi is a traditional yogurt-based drink from the Indian state of punjab.It is thick, creamy, refreshing
and especially popular during the summer. It is often served in a kulhad(clay cup) or a tall glass.

Main Ingredients Fresh curd (yogurt) Cold water or milk Sugar (for sweet lassi) or salt (for salted lassi) Ice cubes Cardamom (optional) Malai (fresh cream) or butter on top Types of Punjabi Lassi 1. Sweet Lassi Made with yogurt, sugar, and ice. Often topped with thick malai. Sometimes flavored with rose water or cardamom. 2. Salted Lassi Made with yogurt, salt, roasted cumin powder, and mint. Refreshing and less sweet. 3. Mango Lassi Sweet lassi blended with ripe mango pulp. Very popular in restaurants. How It Is Made Add thick yogurt to a bowl or blender. Add sugar or salt. Add cold water or milk. Blend until smooth and frothy. Pour into a chilled glass. Garnish with malai, nuts, or saffron if desired. Taste Sweet Lassi: Rich, creamy, and mildly sweet. Salted Lassi: Tangy, refreshing, and savory. Health Benefits Good source of protein and calcium. Contains probiotics that support digestion. Helps keep the body cool in hot weather. Can provide quick energy. Nutritional Value (Approx. 1 Glass – 250 ml) Nutrient Amount Calories 180–250 kcal Protein 7–9 g Carbohydrates 20–30 g Fat 6–10 g Calcium Why is Punjabi Lassi Famous? Thick and creamy texture. Made from fresh dairy, for which Punjab is well known. Served with a generous layer of malai or butter. A traditional drink enjoyed with Punjabi meals like parathas.
